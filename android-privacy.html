LANGUAGE MINER · ANDROID EDITION

Privacy Policy & support

Effective October 14, 2026 · Android policy version 2026-10-14-v2. This policy applies to the Google Play edition of Language Miner, package io.github.erendragneel.languageminer, published under the developer name Eren dragneel. The website's accounts and memberships have a separate privacy policy.

Learning information stays on your device

Your nickname, selected languages, lesson progress, answers, achievements, inventory, settings, and notebook notes are stored inside the app. You do not need an email address or your real name to learn. Local player profiles are not online accounts. Gameplay progress is not sent to the developer, and this edition has no cloud synchronization, online learner linking, chat, or public profile directory.

Pronunciation recordings and game media are included with the game. The app does not contact Azure to generate speech during play and does not use device or cloud speech as a fallback. We have not added an advertising network, gameplay analytics service, or third-party crash-reporting service. Camera, microphone, contacts, advertising-ID, and location permissions are not requested.

Temporary free review demo

Clarification October 15, 2026: Players & backups → Start free review demo, or the toolbar Review demo, opens a separate temporary player held in memory. It provides sample progress and access to optional collections without a password, payment or network connection. This is demonstration access, not a verified subscription, and does not grant membership to your normal player.

Demo progress, currency, ownership and settings are discarded when you reload or select Reset demo. Exit demo returns to normal local players. Demo activities do not write normal player saves; import/export, normal profile changes and device erasure are blocked while demo is active. Exit demo before managing normal profiles or backups. This clarification adds no developer collection or online account.

Disabled subscription purchasing

Subscription checkout is disabled. In this edition's disabled state, the game does not initialize Google Play Billing, request a product catalogue, restore purchases, send purchase tokens to the developer, or open Google Play subscription management. No purchase-verification server is configured. A saved game or imported backup cannot grant paid benefits.

The planned optional memberships renew monthly through Google Play. Before purchases are enabled, we will update this policy with the payment and purchase-verification data processed, the hosting provider, and access-log retention. Google Play will show the local price and applicable taxes before purchase confirmation. Free learning remains available offline.

If you manage a subscription in another release, use Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Removing a local player or uninstalling the game does not cancel a subscription or delete an account held with another service. Google's payment services are governed by Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Free heart-recovery videos

The optional videos describe this game's planned memberships. Completing an eligible video earns one free heart with a six-hour cooldown. No purchase, external advertising network, or ad tracking is involved. Watching a video does not unlock a paid membership.

Backups you choose

You can export and import JSON backups using Android's file picker. Backups contain local progress and may contain notebook notes, so share them carefully. If you choose a cloud-storage provider or another app through the file picker, that provider handles the file under its own privacy policy. Backups cannot grant paid membership access.

Retention, security, and deletion

Local information remains until you remove a player, erase progress, clear app storage, or uninstall the game. Players & backups → Erase all progress removes the local player profiles. Automatic Android backup and device transfer are disabled. Exported files remain in your chosen storage location until you delete them separately.

App files and gameplay are served from the installed package. External web requests from the game display are blocked. Backups are opened or saved only through the file picker you choose. Device security still depends on your Android settings; keep the device and exported backups secure.

The current free Android edition, 6.4.289-play.14, has no Android Internet or network-state permissions. This prevents the app itself from making network requests. An external storage provider you choose through Android's file picker operates separately under its own permissions and privacy policy.

Children and families

Children can learn with local profiles without providing a real name or email address. Younger learners may need adult help with reading and game controls. This edition has no online community links or online teacher sharing. Purchasing and external subscription-management controls remain unavailable while billing is disabled. Parents and guardians should manage any purchases in future releases.

Support and privacy requests

Language Miner · Developer name: Eren dragneel · Support and privacy: languageminersupport@gmail.com.

If you choose to email support, we receive your email address and the information you include. Google provides the support mailbox. We use messages to answer requests and retain them while needed for the request, follow-up, or a legal obligation. You can ask us to delete a support message. Do not send passwords, purchase tokens, bank details, or unnecessary personal information; parents or guardians can contact support for younger learners.

No developer-hosted purchase-verification service is active in this disabled-billing edition. The website and this public policy are hosted on GitHub Pages; visiting them can disclose ordinary web-request information, including an IP address, to GitHub under its privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement. Reading the policy bundled inside the game does not require a website visit.

Changes

We will update the date and policy version when these practices change. Any release that enables online services or purchases must disclose those services before they are used.
