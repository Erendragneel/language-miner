Teaching improvements

Feedback now connects a reviewed rule to the actual example, its translation, and evidence for the answer. Grammar questions specify their intended function. Homophones such as じ / ぢ and ず / づ no longer compete as unequal answers to the same romanized clue.

Pronunciation limitation

409 corrected shared forms lack exact recordings in the existing paid library (818 clips across both voices). Exact recordings remain available for 22,371 forms (98.205%). Separately, the corrected Indonesian letter V clue (fe) lacks both recordings; its former ve recording is not substituted. No new audio was generated, and no obsolete recording was relabeled. Website playback can use an installed device voice; the current bundled-only Android design needs written practice for these gaps. Listening selection skips unavailable clues and offers Reading when none are playable.
