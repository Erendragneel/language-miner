# Illustration production notes

The active picture-to-word mappings are in picture-catalog.js and the coverage report is ARTWORK-COVERAGE.json. Raster illustrations were generated with the built-in image tool and visually reviewed against the vocabulary meaning or a real alphabet example word. Original generated PNGs are preserved.

The working project retains generation queues and review records in artwork-audit. The package includes the active assets and mappings. Precise calendar, quantity, writing-system, and grammar clues are original SVG diagrams. Map diagrams use public-domain Natural Earth geometry, as recorded in ARTWORK-CLUE-NOTES.md.

The general vocabulary art direction is a detailed, warm, painterly adventure-game scene with the meaningful subject on the right and dark navy space on the left for the question. The question identifies the relevant object, action, or sense. Abstract entries include short context where needed.

The following are representative early prompts. The cavern environment is an archived design exploration and is not used as a meaning clue for a letter or word.
