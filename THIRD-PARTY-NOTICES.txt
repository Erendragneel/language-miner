Bundled pronunciation and story audio
This game includes synthesized MP3 pronunciation and story recordings.
NATIVE-VOICES-UPDATE.md records generation using Microsoft Edge's online
neural speech service through the edge-tts client (https://github.com/rany2/edge-tts).
No speech model or edge-tts client is included in the game runtime.
The Android edition plays bundled files without contacting a speech service.
The web edition may also use device/browser speech as a fallback.
The client software license does not establish redistribution rights for
service-generated audio. The release evidence register records the outstanding
confirmation of the applicable generation terms and audio distribution rights.

Background music
Crystal Garden, Lantern Village, and Starlight Library were generated with
Boppy and edited into loops. Prompts, source URLs, dates and processing details
are recorded in audio/MUSIC-SOURCES.md. Terms reference: https://boppy.me/terms.

Historical asset-release status is maintained in ASSET-PROVENANCE.md.
Current packaged-file evidence is generated by android-release/scripts/release-evidence.cjs.
