## Free search and evidence work still available

1. Define actual release territories, goods/services and exact name/logo variants in use.
2. Search USPTO registered/pending federal records for exact and similar spellings, sounds, meanings and designs. Save dated queries, relevant records, owners, goods/services and current status. Include the Trademark Official Gazette and common-law use.
3. Check state/business registries, app stores, public web use and domains, plus relevant foreign registers for intended territories. Preserve results; domain/app-name availability does not establish trademark rights.
4. Compare related goods/services and overall commercial impression. Class numbers can organize searches but a different class alone does not eliminate conflict.
5. Review the actual logo, characters and visual presentation separately from wording. Document unresolved matches and obtain qualified interpretation if material uncertainty remains.

USPTO describes a comprehensive search across multiple resources for similar marks used with related goods/services. Similarity can involve sound, appearance, meaning and commercial impression. Buying a registration or paid search is not a universal condition for releasing an app. This project has not completed the search/assessment above.

## Registration and symbols

Federal registration is optional and provides additional protection. USPTO permits TM for goods or SM for services even without an application. The federal registration symbol requires a registration and applies only to covered goods/services. No registration is established here, and this update does not add symbols to the game.

An experienced attorney/search service can help assess complex findings. That is one way to resolve uncertainty, not evidence of clearance or an automatic fee required by the source checker. Preserve first-use evidence and final artwork whether or not an application is filed.
