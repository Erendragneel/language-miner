Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026 · This policy explains the information used by the Language Miner web game, its cloud account service, optional Patreon connection, and Parent/Teacher Center.

Return to gameTerms of ServiceThird-party notices
Plain-language summary: Language Miner does not sell personal information or include behavioral-advertising trackers. Direct accounts are for people age 13 or older. The service stores account and learning-progress data needed to operate the game. A signed-in player can export data, submit a private request, or delete the account from Player → Privacy & Safety.

1. Scope and operator

This policy applies to Language Miner at erendragneel.github.io/language-miner and its connected Supabase services. “Language Miner,” “we,” and “the service” refer to the independent Language Miner project.

For support or privacy inquiries, email languageminersupport@gmail.com.

For a private account-data request, sign in and open Player → Privacy & Safety. That authenticated workflow connects the request to the correct account without requiring personal information in a public issue or message.

Launch requirement: The operator must add the legally required business contact details after an attorney confirms the operating entity. Do not market the service to children under 13 or schools until that review is complete.

2. Age requirements

Language Miner does not currently create direct accounts for children under 13. Signup asks for an age category, not a date of birth. Choosing “under 13” stops the signup before an account request is sent.

A parent or teacher account does not bypass this restriction or automatically create verified parental consent. If we learn that a direct account belongs to a child under 13, we may suspend or delete it while handling an appropriate parent request. A separate verified-parental-consent system must be launched before direct under-13 accounts are offered.

4. Where information is stored

  • On the player’s device: cached application files, active profile information, safety snapshots, Parent/Teacher links, and local gameplay/course copies.
  • Supabase: login account, revisioned cloud save, consent record, privacy requests, administration status, and an optional Patreon connection.
  • GitHub Pages: public game files. GitHub may process ordinary web-hosting request information under its own policies.
  • Patreon: Patreon independently manages subscriptions and payment information. Only limited membership information requested through the connection is returned to Language Miner.
  • Device speech services: pronunciation uses browser or operating-system speech features. The platform provider may process the spoken text according to its settings and policies.

5. How information is used

  • Provide accounts, cloud saves, course progress, assessments, rewards, accessibility, and recovery.
  • Verify Patreon membership benefits after a player chooses to connect Patreon.
  • Provide learner-approved, read-only Parent/Teacher summaries.
  • Protect the service, diagnose failures, and enforce the Terms.
  • Answer authenticated privacy and account requests.

Language Miner does not use account data for targeted advertising and does not sell personal information.

6. Sharing and processors

Information is disclosed only as necessary to service providers such as Supabase, GitHub Pages, Patreon after optional connection, and the player’s browser/platform speech service; when required by law; or to protect users and the service. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Approved adults see only learner summaries described in the Parent/Teacher Center. They cannot answer questions, spend Nuggets, reset progress, or read private Notebook notes through that center.

7. Retention and deletion

Cloud account and save information is retained while the account is active so progress can be restored. Privacy-request records are retained while needed to resolve and document the request. Local information remains until the player deletes it, clears site storage, removes the local profile, or deletes the account.

Delete account in Privacy & Safety removes the Supabase Auth user and associated cloud save, Patreon connection, consent, OAuth state, and privacy requests through database cascade rules. It does not cancel a Patreon subscription. Backups previously downloaded by the player remain under that player’s control.

8. Player choices and rights

Depending on location, a player may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to, or receive a copy of personal information. Privacy & Safety provides an immediate JSON export and an authenticated request form. Players may disconnect Patreon separately. Learners can approve, decline, or revoke Parent/Teacher links.

9. Security

Language Miner uses HTTPS, Supabase authentication, row-level security or restricted security-definer functions, server-held secrets, signed Patreon webhooks, short-lived OAuth state, account-scoped exports, and confirmation before deletion. No system can guarantee absolute security. Players should use a unique password, keep devices secured, and never submit passwords in feedback or privacy requests.

10. International use and changes

The game can be accessed internationally, so information may be processed where service providers operate. Additional notices or contractual safeguards may be required before actively marketing in particular countries or to schools.

Material policy changes will receive a new version and may require renewed acknowledgement in the game. The effective date at the top identifies the current version.
