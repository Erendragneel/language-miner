Language Miner — player-specific Daily Golem model

The armature and weighted finger topology derive from Quaternius Universal Base Characters [Standard], CC0 1.0:
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Head, swept hair, torso, sleeves, forearms, trousers, collar and boots were rebuilt for Language Miner's existing player artwork. Front/back textures were generated with OpenAI ImageGen using the customized in-game player as the reference (2026-09-24). The generic peasant outfit and superhero head are not used. See tools/golem-3d/player-textures/PROMPTS.md.
